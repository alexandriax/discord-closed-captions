Privacy and consent While captions are on, the extension sends the Discord tab's rendered audio—and your microphone only if you enable it—directly to OpenAI for transcription. It never saves audio. The optional latest-session transcript remains in extension-local storage until replaced or cleared. Tell everyone in the call and get their consent first.
